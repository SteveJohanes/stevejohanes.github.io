Explore Tahura Adventure

Explore Tahura Adventure is a mobile app that gives visitors of Taman Hutan Raya (Tahura) an interactive and educational experience. It combines information, navigation, safety features, and game elements, so visitors can explore the area in a more fun way while learning about its flora, fauna, and history.

Type
Application
Built with
Flutter, PostgreSQL, Supabase

Goals

  • Provide educational information about Tahura’s flora, fauna, and history.
  • Increase visitor engagement through gamification.
  • Make navigation easier with an interactive map.
  • Improve visitor safety with emergency assistance.

Key features

  • Login, registration, and user profile with visit history and achievements
  • Online ticket and merchandise ordering with a QR e-ticket
  • QR check-in and QR scanning to learn about flora and fauna
  • Digital flora and fauna cards, leaderboard, and badges
  • Interactive map with real-time GPS navigation
  • Challenges and missions that earn points
  • Emergency button and safety information
  • Notifications, feedback, and issue reporting
